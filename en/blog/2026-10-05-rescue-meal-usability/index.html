Worklog: Rescue Meal Usability Fixes

A record of the day I fixed the home, meal plan, and shopping flows at each moment of use.

Rescue Meal is an ingredient app that tells you which ingredients to use first. It upgrades class work, aimed at one- or two-person households who gave up on management apps because registering food was tedious. On this day I fixed friction by moment of use and tidied the TestFlight release flow. As in checking the launched GS App, I went by the order of use rather than the feature list.

What I did

Moment of use What I fixed
First registration Name, quantity, and storage location only, no receipt
Home Tapping fridge, freezer, or pantry counts opens that list directly
Choosing meals Strict time limits. Before, picking 10 minutes could still show longer recipes
Recommendation score Registering one ingredient in parts pushed the match rate past 100%; now calculated per recipe ingredient
Purchase registration No saving before choosing storage, and decimals like 1000.5g save as entered

Where I got stuck

The stricter time condition broke shopping recalculation for previously saved meal plans. Allowing the saved actual cooking time when recalculating the same saved recipe resolved it. A separate review flagged inventory freshness and this compatibility issue; I fixed both and passed re-review.

New recommendations

The time limit is enforced strictly.

Recalculating saved plans

The recipe was already chosen, so the saved actual cooking time is allowed.

What I checked

Tests for recommendation, meal plan, shopping, and stock-in flows plus regression tests for the new screens confirmed this change. The purchase path was checked with real requests and the detail screen. Existing failures in broader API tests were separated for later.

What’s next

Device camera and OCR accuracy, push delivery, production deployment, and real users’ return visits and food savings weren’t covered. The next day I fixed PC Supporter’s power delivery verdict.
