Worklog: Rescue Meal Usability Fixes

A record of the day I fixed the home, meal plan, and shopping flows at each moment of use.

Rescue Meal is a grocery management app that tells you which ingredients to use first. On this day I fixed pain points at each moment of use and cleaned up the TestFlight release flow.

What I Did

  • Made the first food item registrable with just a name, quantity, and storage location, without a receipt.
  • Tapping the refrigerated, frozen, or room-temperature count on the home screen now opens that list directly.
  • Made the time limit strict. Before, picking 10 minutes could still return a recipe that took longer when nothing fit.
  • Fixed a calculation where registering the same ingredient in separate entries pushed the fulfillment rate above 100% and inflated the score, so it now works per recipe ingredient.
  • In purchase registration, saving is blocked until a storage location is chosen, and decimals like 1000.5g are saved as they are.

What Blocked Me

Because of the stricter time condition, recalculating the shopping list for previously saved meal plans failed. I solved it by allowing the saved actual cooking time when recalculating the same saved menu. A separate review flagged inventory freshness and this compatibility problem, and after I fixed them it passed re-review.

What I Checked

I ran tests for the recommendation, meal plan, shopping, and receiving flows plus new screen regression tests to confirm this change. I checked the purchase path through actual requests and the detail screen. Existing failures left in the broader API tests were separated from this change to be handled on their own.

What’s Left

Real-device camera and OCR accuracy, push delivery, production deployment, and actual users’ return visits and food-cost savings were not included in this verification.
