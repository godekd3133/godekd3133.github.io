Worklog: Career Center MVP

The day I built the first version of AI mock interviews and aptitude test practice, along with the gaps that a later check exposed.

I built the MVP of the AI mock interview and aptitude test practice app.

What I Did

I split the code into three layers.

  • packages/core: AI response schema, Korean speech analysis (fillers, repetition, pauses, speaking rate, time), personality testing (consistency, exaggerated responses), generative aptitude tests, multiple-choice onboarding
  • apps/server: APIs for researching company values and past exam questions, generating questions, evaluating answers, and producing an overall summary
  • apps/web: onboarding, experience cards, document upload, voice interview room, report, aptitude test screens, PWA

I added reading questions aloud, captions, follow-up questions, and hands-free mode to the voice interview room, and document consistency, answer-again comparison, and trends to the report. I also wrote the design, roadmap, and competitive analysis documents.

Decisions

  • Instead of fixing on a single AI provider, I wrapped four of them, up to a subscription CLI, the Claude API, and a trial mode, behind the same interface. Every provider answers with the same JSON schema. Even without a key, the trial mode lets the screens run.
  • Speech metrics aren’t left to the AI. They are computed in core.
  • By keeping platform-shared logic in core, I made room to attach desktop and mobile apps later.

What Blocked Me

The gaps showed up a few days later.

  • October 16: I added a goals stage and a gap diagnosis based on job postings. The MVP didn’t have this flow.
  • October 18: After adding job posting selection, device sync, and desktop and mobile apps, I ran a technical check. That is when I fixed data loss in sync and storage, microphone cleanup, accessibility, and server security issues. Ahead of going public, I also added a subscription CLI block and internal address blocking (SSRF).

What’s Left

The MVP had only been confirmed to the point of working. I later checked the questions with AI virtual personas and have not yet validated them with real users.
