Working While Going to University

A record of enrolling in the Department of Media Management at Soongsil University while working at Bagelcode, and of how coursework turned into personal projects.

(Written in 2026, looking back.)

I finished my service as Skilled Industrial Personnel (alternative military service) on December 23, 2023, and in February 2024 I enrolled in the Department of Media Management at Soongsil University. I’ve kept my job at Bagelcode and am doing both. I’m also active in Gammaru, a game development club.

DangunMyth in My First Semester

I was the development lead on DangunMyth, a project based on the Dangun founding myth.

  • April 22, 2024: I wrote the first code in Processing.
  • April 29: I added an image manager and split up the file structure.
  • May 7: I added a time manager and an animation system.
  • June 9: The team merged the p5.js conversion work and began moving to the web.
  • June 17: I finished the p5.js conversion.

At work I was using TypeScript and Unity, while the school project started in Processing and moved to p5.js.
