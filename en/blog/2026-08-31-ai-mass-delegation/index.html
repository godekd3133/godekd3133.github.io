Mass Delegation: Handing Most of My Work to AI

Around July 2026 I began handing most of my work to AI, and missed verification led me to change the completion condition.

(Written in 2026, looking back.)

Around July 2026 I began handing most of my work to AI. This is also around when I started building an internal AI tool to help with slot development.

In August I also started using automated review and hook settings. I spent less time typing code myself and more time splitting up tasks and checking the results.

Delegating this much exposed a problem: verification after edits often got skipped. So I changed the completion condition from the agent’s report to an actual run result.
