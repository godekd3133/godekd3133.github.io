Starting My First Job as a High School Student

A record of joining 9M Interactive in my final year of high school and working on the core and unit AI of a card-deck merge battle game.

(Written in 2026, looking back.)

In July 2020, my final year of high school, I joined 9M Interactive as a junior client programmer. I was on the Sheed team. I graduated from Seoul Digitech High School in March 2021, and I stayed at the company until December 3, 2021.

What I Worked On

I worked on a card-deck-based merge battle game.

  • Implemented the simulation core for the card-deck merge battle
  • Implemented combat unit AI and crowd movement
  • Developed the out-of-game UI

Before and After Joining

The year I joined, 2020, my high school activities overlapped with the job. That year I took 3rd place at the Regional Skills Competition and 5th place at the National Skills Competition, and I released Bright Burn and Ttangpaseo Donnaonya?! (Dig for Money?!). With Jack’s Bean, a team I formed with friends, I made Morbido and Corona Yellow, and we won the Grand Prize at the Employment UP! Game Development Competition for IT Vocational High Schools. The five Jack’s Bean teammates were high school friends who were working at game companies at the time.

From February 2021 I served as Skilled Industrial Personnel (alternative military service), and I finished my service on December 23, 2023.

I also formed a separate team and made mobile games, and this is when I learned to integrate services such as PlayFab, BACKND, and Google Play Games. MTalk, a TCP chat program, is also from this period.

What I Wrote Down at the Time

After joining, I wrote this down: meeting many people in the industry helped me mature as a person and grow further as a programmer, and after pouring myself into one project for a year, my doubts about a career as a game developer turned into certainty.
