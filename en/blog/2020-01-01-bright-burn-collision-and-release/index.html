Bright Burn: Collision Handling and Release

Notes on collision handling, map construction, exhibitions, and release for Bright Burn, a vertical-scrolling platformer action game I worked on for six months.

(Written in 2026, looking back.)

Bright Burn is a vertical-scrolling platformer action game that I made over six months with Oh! Rock, my game development team in high school. I was in charge of the player, the shop, enemies, UI, and the Google and AdMob integrations.

Four-Direction Platform Collision

To handle collisions between the four-direction platforms and enemies, I didn’t use a single Collider. I attached Left, Right, Top, and Bottom child objects around the outside of the player and added one more at the center, so I could tell which side had been hit.

Bullet Through Paper

A fast-moving object can pass through a thin object between two frames, so the collision never registers. To prevent this in collisions between the player and objects, I used BoxCast.

Slow Motion and Maps

I handled slow motion by managing Game Speed separately, and I expressed night and day with a Diffuse shader and Lights. I also reduced draw calls with atlas images.

The maps were built by joining tilemap pieces together. Based on the player’s position, only three slots are managed: Main, SubUp, and SubDown. When the player reaches the end, the next map is picked at random. This is the part that re-rolls so the map currently in use doesn’t come up again.

do
{
    i_SubUp = Random.Range(0, Maps.Count);
} while (i_SubUp == i_Main || i_SubUp == i_SubDown);
SubUp = Maps[i_SubUp];
SubUp.transform.position = new Vector3(0, 100 * (PlayerMapIndex + 1), 0);

Once the score passes 2000, Normal maps are added to the candidate list, and past 3000, Hard maps are added.

Exhibitions and Release

In 2019 we exhibited at the Global Game Challenge, G-Star, and Gamescom, and we also showed it, together with Under Watch, at an exhibition for Smilegate employees. It was released in 2020. I founded Oh! Rock and ran two projects with six teammates.

The gameplay video is on YouTube.
