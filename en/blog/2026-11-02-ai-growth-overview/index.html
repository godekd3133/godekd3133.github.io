How Has My Way of Working with AI Tools Changed?

Based on my use of Codex and Claude Code, I divided the period from August 2025 to now into five stages.

I looked back over how I have used Codex and Claude Code. The biggest turning point came around July 2026, when I began handing most of my work to AI.

Five Stages

Period Stage What changed
Aug 2025 – Feb 2026 Exploration A time of trying out the tools.
Mar – Jun 2026 Into daily work First used skills, subagents, MCP, and parallel agents.
Jul – Aug 2026 Mass delegation Began handing most of my work to AI and splitting it across many sessions.
Sep 2026 Stronger verification The share of running tests and builds after edits grew sharply.
Oct 2026 – Running in parallel, many products Using two tools together, and widening my scope through personal products.

One Problem and What I Did About It

As I delegated more, verification after edits often got skipped. So I changed the completion condition to an actual run result, and reports now separate how far things were actually run and checked.
