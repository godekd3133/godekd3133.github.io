Worklog: Reactor Rush Change of Direction

A record of the day I stopped polishing the free-for-all game and swapped the default entry point for a single-player expedition.

After changing the default game three times in days, on this day I moved to a single-player expedition.

10.03 Spark Raid first version10.05 Rebuilt as a core heist10.06 75-second 3D bounty duel10.07 Single-player expedition

On October 5 I judged graphics, fun, presentation, and lobby all lacking, and on October 6 I judged the core heist version lacking in fun, feel, and graphics.

What I did

On October 7 I first fixed the duel: a skippable control warm-up, showing the opponent’s current aim, and marking the central charge cell. I also fixed a charge cell defect that skipped the next spawn cycle because of lifetime processing order.

Still, I judged the duel lacking in graphics, lobby, presentation, and core, and replaced the default entry point entirely with the Reactor Rush single-player expedition: pass three zones and defeat a boss.

Where I got stuck

The duel’s technical checks were passing. Most checklist feature items were done and confirmed in a real browser, but I couldn’t call it fun. Passing checks and being a good game are different things, and that’s where I got stuck.

What checks tell you

Features work, input registers, and there are no errors.

What they don't

Whether you want to play again, whether it feels good, whether the graphics are good.

Judgment

  • Not going back to small duel fixes.
  • Not using past QA passes as grounds for the new direction.
  • Not deleting old files and records. Bounty and Heist sources stay for history, and a new save key leaves existing wallet saves untouched.

What’s next

The new expedition’s first verification is at the level of browser input and simulation. I found and fixed an early lobby character showing its back and a cropped framing, and confirmed a boss win with real input. Multi-touch on physical devices, audio, and human fun evaluation weren’t verified at this point. More in What I Learned Building Reactor Rush.
