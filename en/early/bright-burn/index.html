Early games

Bright Burn

Unity, 2019

Vertical scrolling, Platform action, Built in 6 months, Released, G-Star, Gamescom

My part

Player, Shop, Enemies, UI, Google and AdMob integration

What I did

  • Split the player's colliders across the four outer sides and the center to manage collisions with platforms and enemies in four directions.
  • Used BoxCast to prevent bullet-through-paper in collisions between the player and objects.
  • Managed game speed independently to handle slow motion flexibly.
  • Used a diffuse shader and lights to depict day and night.
