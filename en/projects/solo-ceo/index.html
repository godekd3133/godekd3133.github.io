Solo CEO

A back office that helps small business owners with grants and tax preparation

In development, Service, Sep 2026 ~ Oct 2026

Open

Runs without a server, using synthetic business data. Nothing is actually filed or submitted.

The problem

Running a shop on your own means missing grants you could receive and losing time to tax preparation.

What I did

  • Implemented an Expo-based monorepo
  • Wrote the OpenAPI spec and a requirements traceability matrix
  • Drafted the service scope ahead of legal review, leaving out filing on a user's behalf

Tech stack

  • Expo
  • Python
  • Docker
  • GCP
Development log

How it started

It grew out of the Solo CEO business plan submitted for a Fourth Industrial Revolution and Entrepreneurship course. I turned the idea of an AI back office that helps solo business owners find grants and handle taxes into a real service.
